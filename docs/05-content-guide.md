# Content Guide — Page by Page

Voice: warm, plain-spoken, specific. Lead with what the customer gets. Use numbers. Never
open a sentence with "Veneta™ has" or "Veneta™ offers". Trademark symbols appear on the first
mention in body copy only, never mid-heading.

## Product page template (apply to all eight categories)

1. **H1** — the category name, plain. "Cellular Shades".
2. **Opening line (1 sentence, benefit first)** — e.g. "Insulating honeycomb cells that cut heat loss at the window and soften the light coming through it."
3. **Meta row** — From $XX at The Home Depot · star rating · badges.
4. **Best for / Consider something else if** — 3-4 items each. Be honest; this is the trust engine.
   - *Cellular, best for:* energy savings, bedrooms needing blackout, skylights and French doors, homes wanting a clean cordless look.
   - *Consider something else if:* you want an unobstructed view when lowered (choose Solar), or a traditional wood look (choose Faux Wood).
5. **Signature features** — max four, each one image and one claim.
6. **Colours & fabrics** — grouped by opacity, with the free-sample nudge.
7. **Options & sizes** — the spec table. Preserve every existing dimension from the current site exactly.
8. **How to measure** — short version plus a link to the full guide.
9. **FAQ** — reuse the existing (excellent) answers, lightly edited for voice.
10. **Related products** — three, with a one-line reason for each.
11. **Closing CTA band** — Shop + Order free samples.

## SEO title and description patterns

- Product: `Cellular Shades — Cordless & Motorized Custom Shades | VENETA™` / "Insulating cellular shades in 3/8" to 1 1/4" cells, cordless or motorized, in widths from 8.5" to 120". See sizes, fabrics and how to measure."
- Room: `Best Blinds & Shades for [Room] | VENETA™`
- Need: `Blackout Shades for Bedrooms | VENETA™`
- Support: `How to Measure for Blinds & Shades (Inside & Outside Mount) | VENETA™`

Every description is hand-written, 140-158 characters, and contains a concrete number or a
specific benefit. Never auto-generated from page text.

## Journal relaunch (blog)

Two posts per month, three pillars:
- **Choose** — "Blackout vs. room darkening: what actually blocks light", "Which shade fits a skylight".
- **Live** — "How to clean cellular shades without creasing them", "Cutting summer cooling costs at the window".
- **Design** — colour and trend pieces, each linking to at least two product pages.

Every post ends with a related-product block and a Home Depot CTA. Retire or update the
2020-era posts rather than leaving them to date the site.
