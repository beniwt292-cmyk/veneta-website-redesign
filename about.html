About

We make window coverings that fit properly.

Veneta is the window fashions brand of Richfield Window Coverings. We build made-to-measure blinds, shades and shutters in 1/8" increments, ship them cordless, and stand behind them for the life of the product.

See the rangeHow we build them

What we do

Eight product lines, all made to order, all sold through The Home Depot. Nothing in our range is a stock size, because almost no window is a stock size.

Three things we decided early

Cordless on everything

Not as a premium option or a separate safety line. There is no looped cord on any product we make, which is the simplest way to meet a safety standard: remove the hazard.

Cut to 1/8 inch

Rounding to the nearest half inch is cheaper to manufacture and it is why so many shades have an inconsistent light gap. We cut ClearFit™ headrails to the opening you give us.

Say what the product does not do

A solar screen does not give you night privacy. A blackout Roman shade still leaks light at the edges. A motorized shade needs a hub for voice control. Telling you that before you buy costs us a sale occasionally and saves a return every time.

Where we sit in the market

Between the stock sizes on a shelf and a full custom workroom. You get made-to-measure manufacturing and a limited lifetime warranty, at retail prices, with the retailer handling the transaction and us handling the product.

Richfield Window Coverings

Veneta is one of several brands operated by Richfield Window Coverings, a manufacturer of residential window treatments for the North American market. Trade and volume enquiries are handled through our professionals team.
