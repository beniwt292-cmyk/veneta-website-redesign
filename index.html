Engineered to fit

Every dimension, published.

Custom means measured once and fitted right. We publish the exact width and height range for every cell size and lift system, so you know it works before you order.

Including the windows nobody else covers: skylights, arches, French doors and 10-foot sliders.

Family safety

Cordless by design, because kids and pets don't read labels.

  • Cordless lift is the standard across every product line, not a paid upgrade.
  • Roman shades are cord-free on the front and the back.
  • Tested to industry safety standards for homes with children and pets.
  • Motorized options remove the pull entirely on hard-to-reach windows.

Motorization

Raise every shade in the house without getting up.

Quiet, rechargeable motors with remote, wall-switch or hub control. No wiring, no dangling cords, no batteries to buy.

01

ShadeAuto™ Hub

TruQuiet™ motors

RevitaCharge™
