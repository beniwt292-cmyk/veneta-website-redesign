Why roman shades

Drapery fabric, held in a fold that repeats.

Fabric, with the structure kept

A flat fold hangs almost sheer against the frame. A hobbled fold holds a soft stack of curves even when it is fully lowered.

The lining decides the room

Unlined for light, privacy lining for a street-facing window, blackout lining for a bedroom. The face fabric does not change.

Cord-free front and back

Most fabric shades hide a cord ladder behind the panel. Ours does not, which is the reason a Roman shade can go in a nursery at all.

Child & pet safety

No cord in reach.

Cordless lift is standard on this line, not a paid upgrade. Corded window coverings remain a strangulation hazard for young children, which is why the cordless option is the default here and not an option you have to find.

Guidance published by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends cordless window coverings in any home with young children.

FAQ

Asked often, answered plainly.

If the answer you need is not here, the full list covers every line.

All questions
Are Roman shades cordless?

Yes. Cordless lift is the standard configuration; motorization is available on every fabric.

How much depth does a Roman shade need to mount inside the frame?

2 1/2" for a flush inside mount, because the fabric stack sits behind the headrail. Shallow frames should use an outside mount.

Can I wash a Roman shade?

No. Dust it, spot clean gently, and use a professional cleaner for anything larger. Washing removes the sizing that holds the folds.

How much light comes in around the edges?

With an inside mount, expect a light gap of about 1/4" on each side. Choose an outside mount or add SmartPrivacy® channels on a blackout roller if you need a true dark room.
