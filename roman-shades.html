Overview

Roman shades are the choice when a window needs fabric rather than hardware. The panel stacks into even folds as it rises and hangs as a single clean plane when lowered. All Veneta Roman shades are made to order with a rigid batten in each fold so the lines stay crisp after years of use.

Options

What you choose

Flat fold or hobbled fold

Flat folds read tailored and modern. Hobbled folds keep a soft cascade even when the shade is fully lowered, which suits traditional trim and deeper window frames.

Lining you choose

Unlined for maximum light, privacy lining for everyday rooms, blackout lining for bedrooms. The lining is sewn in, not clipped on.

Cordless lift

A cordless lift system hides inside the headrail. There is no cord to route, tie off or keep away from a crib.

Fabric library

Linen blends, textured cottons and performance weaves that resist fading on a bright window. Order swatches before you commit.

Before you order

Measure once, properly.

01

Choose the mount

Inside mount sits within the frame for a built-in look. Outside mount covers the whole opening and blocks more light.

02

Measure width in three places

Top, middle and bottom. Use the narrowest number for an inside mount.

03

Measure height in three places

Left, centre and right. Use the longest number for an inside mount.

04

Do not deduct anything

Give us the exact opening. We make the deductions for you.

The rule that saves most orders: record measurements to the nearest 1/8" and never round down on height. The full walkthrough, with diagrams, is in the measuring guide.

FAQ

4 questions, answered honestly.

Are Roman shades cordless?

Yes. Cordless lift is the standard configuration; motorization is available on every fabric.

How much depth does a Roman shade need to mount inside the frame?

2 1/2" for a flush inside mount, because the fabric stack sits behind the headrail. Shallow frames should use an outside mount.

Can I wash a Roman shade?

No. Dust it, spot clean gently, and use a professional cleaner for anything larger. Washing removes the sizing that holds the folds.

How much light comes in around the edges?

With an inside mount, expect a light gap of about 1/4" on each side. Choose an outside mount or add SmartPrivacy® channels on a blackout roller if you need a true dark room.
