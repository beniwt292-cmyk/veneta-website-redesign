Trends 2026

What is actually changing.

Six shifts we are seeing in orders, not six things we would like to sell you.

Trend 01

Warm neutrals over cool grey

Greige and sand have replaced stark white and cold grey as the default. They work with both warm oak and cool tile, which is what an open plan room needs.

Trend 02

Texture instead of pattern

A visible weave catches light and reads relaxed. Printed pattern on a window dates faster than anything else in a room.

Trend 03

Layering a screen and a blackout

Two shades on one window: a 3% solar screen for the day, blackout for the evening. Increasingly standard in bedrooms.

Trend 04

Wider slats and louvres

The 2 1/2" slat and the 4 1/2" louvre. Fewer lines across the glass, more view when open.

Trend 05

Full-height glazing treated as one plane

Wide-span tracks so a wall of glass reads as one continuous treatment rather than four separate shades.

Trend 06

Quiet motorization

Schedules matter more than voice control. Shades that close before the room heats up, without anyone thinking about it.
