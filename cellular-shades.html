Cellular Shades

The warmest shade in the line.

Honeycomb cells trap air at the window, so rooms hold their temperature and stay quieter. Choose single cell for value or double cell for the highest insulation, in light filtering or full blackout.

Overview

Cellular shades are the fastest way to fix a cold, bright or noisy window. The honeycomb structure creates a pocket of still air between the glass and the room, which slows heat transfer in both directions. Every Veneta cellular shade ships cordless as standard, with a ClearFit™ headrail cut to your exact opening.

Options

What you choose

Single or double cell

Single cell gives you a slimmer stack and a lower price. Double cell adds a second air pocket for the best insulation and sound absorption on the wall you notice most.

Light filtering or blackout

Light filtering diffuses daylight into an even glow. Blackout adds a foil liner behind the fabric and pairs with SmartPrivacy® side channels for bedrooms and nurseries.

Top-down/bottom-up

Lower the top rail for daylight with privacy at eye level. Useful on street-facing windows and ground-floor bathrooms.

Cordless by design

No looped cord, no cleats, no tension devices. The shade holds position anywhere you leave it and meets the current cordless safety standard for homes with children and pets.

Before you order

Measure once, properly.

01

Choose the mount

Inside mount sits within the frame for a built-in look. Outside mount covers the whole opening and blocks more light.

02

Measure width in three places

Top, middle and bottom. Use the narrowest number for an inside mount.

03

Measure height in three places

Left, centre and right. Use the longest number for an inside mount.

04

Do not deduct anything

Give us the exact opening. We make the deductions for you.

The rule that saves most orders: record measurements to the nearest 1/8" and never round down on height. The full walkthrough, with diagrams, is in the measuring guide.

FAQ

5 questions, answered honestly.

Do cellular shades really reduce heating and cooling costs?

They reduce heat transfer at the glass, which is where most homes lose the most energy. A double cell blackout shade insulates best. Savings depend on your window, orientation and climate, so we do not publish a single percentage.

Are these shades cordless?

Yes. Every cellular shade we make is cordless as standard. There is no looped cord anywhere on the product.

Can I motorize a cellular shade?

Yes, with TruQuiet™ motorization. Smart-home support depends on the motor and hub combination you choose, so check the compatibility list before you buy.

How do I clean them?

Dust with a soft brush attachment on low suction. Spot clean with a barely damp cloth and blot dry. Do not soak or submerge a blackout shade, because the liner can separate.

What is the minimum window depth?

3/4" for a flush inside mount. Anything shallower should use an outside mount, which we cover in the measuring guide.
