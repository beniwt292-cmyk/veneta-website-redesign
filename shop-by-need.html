Shop by need

Name the problem. We'll name the product.

Most people arrive with a problem, not a product in mind: the sun hits the screen, the bedroom is too bright, the patio door looks bare. Start there.

Use the product finderShop by room

Need 01

Block the light.

Blackout fabrics plus SmartPrivacy® channels to close the edge gap.

Recommended lines: Cellular Shades, Roller & Solar, Roman Shades.

Need 02

Cut glare and heat.

Solar screens rated 1% to 5% openness for west-facing rooms and screens.

Recommended lines: Roller & Solar, Sheer Shades.

Need 03

Keep the view.

10% and 14% screens and wide-louvre shutters that open right up.

Recommended lines: Roller & Solar, Shutters.

Need 04

Child and pet safe.

Cordless lift on every product, no exceptions.

Recommended lines: Cellular Shades, Sheer Shades, Faux Wood Blinds.

Need 05

Lower the energy bill.

Double cell cellular shades and framed shutters seal the window best.

Recommended lines: Cellular Shades, Shutters.

Need 06

Cover a patio door.

Wide-span tracks that rotate for light and slide aside for access.

Recommended lines: DualDrape™, Vertical Blinds.

Need 07

Handle humidity.

Composite faux wood and vinyl that will not warp above a bath or sink.

Recommended lines: Faux Wood Blinds, Vertical Blinds.

Need 08

Quiet the room.

Honeycomb cells and fabric vanes absorb reflected sound.

Recommended lines: Cellular Shades, Roman Shades.
