Overview

DualDrape™ solves the problem every wide sliding door has: you want the softness of drapery, the light control of a vertical blind, and a way to actually walk outside. Fabric vanes rotate for light and privacy, then the whole panel traverses to one side, stacking tight against the jamb.

Options

What you choose

Rotate, then traverse

Two independent movements from one wand: tilt the vanes for light, then pull the panel aside to open the door.

Patio door scale

Built to 192" wide in a single track, so a whole wall of glass reads as one continuous treatment.

Removable, washable vanes

Vanes unclip individually. Wash the one the dog leaned on instead of replacing the whole treatment.

Split stack

Stack left, right or split to the centre depending on which way your door slides.

Before you order

Measure once, properly.

01

Choose the mount

Inside mount sits within the frame for a built-in look. Outside mount covers the whole opening and blocks more light.

02

Measure width in three places

Top, middle and bottom. Use the narrowest number for an inside mount.

03

Measure height in three places

Left, centre and right. Use the longest number for an inside mount.

04

Do not deduct anything

Give us the exact opening. We make the deductions for you.

The rule that saves most orders: record measurements to the nearest 1/8" and never round down on height. The full walkthrough, with diagrams, is in the measuring guide.

FAQ

4 questions, answered honestly.

Will DualDrape™ work on a French door?

On a wide French opening, yes, with a ceiling mount and a split stack. On a single hinged door, a shade mounted to the door itself is usually better.

How far does the stack project?

Roughly 6" to 14" depending on width. Plan for the stack next to the jamb, not over the glass.

Can I wash the vanes at home?

Yes. Unclip a vane, hand wash cool, hang to dry flat, and re-clip. Do not machine dry.

Is there a cord anywhere on this product?

No. Rotation and traverse are both handled by a rigid wand.
