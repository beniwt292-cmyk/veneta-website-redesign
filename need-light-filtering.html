Need · Light filtering

Keep the daylight. Lose the glare.

Most rooms do not need to be dark. They need the hard edge taken off the light, so the room stays bright without a stripe of sun across the floor.

How it works

The mechanism, before the products.

Opacity and openness are not the same thing

Opacity describes how much light a fabric passes: light filtering, room darkening, blackout. Openness describes how much of the fabric is physically hole: a 3% screen is 3% open weave. A light filtering fabric glows. An open screen lets you see through it.

Diffusion is what you are buying

A light filtering fabric turns one bright source into a whole illuminated surface. The room reads brighter than the light meter says, because the glow arrives from a large area instead of a small one. That is also why it kills glare on a screen or a glossy table.

Privacy in daylight, silhouette at night

Any fabric that lets light through in one direction lets it through in the other. A light filtering shade is private in daylight and becomes a silhouette after dark once the room lights are on. If that matters, look at the privacy page.

Colour changes the answer

A pale fabric diffuses and brightens. A dark fabric of the same opacity absorbs and preserves contrast, which is why a charcoal screen gives you a sharper view out. Same fabric spec, completely different room.

What we would fit

Three that actually work.

Ranked, with the downside written next to each one.

Pick 01 · Cellular Shades

Light filtering honeycomb, for an even glow

The most consistent diffuser in the range. Light entering the honeycomb bounces inside the cell before it reaches the room, so the whole shade lights up evenly with no hot spot.

The tradeoff. You cannot see out of it at all. If the window has a view you care about, this is the wrong line and a 10% screen is the right one.

Cellular Shades

Pick 02 · Sheer Shades

Fabric vanes between two sheers

The one that gives you both. Open the vanes and you have a soft view through the knit facings; close them and the room diffuses. Up to 88% UV blocked with the vanes open.

The tradeoff. It needs 2 1/2" of depth for an inside mount and it is the most delicate fabric here. It also cannot get properly dark, whatever you do with the vanes.

Sheer Shades

Pick 03 · Roman Shades

Unlined or privacy-lined fabric

When you want the light softened and the window dressed at the same time. An unlined linen glows; a privacy lining takes it a stop darker without going opaque.

The tradeoff. Romans stack in front of the glass, so the top of the window is never fully clear. Care is dust and spot clean only, which rules them out over a sink.

Straight answer

What will not get you there.

The range has eight lines. Some of them are the wrong answer here, and it is cheaper to read that than to return an order.

  • Blackout anything. It is the opposite specification, and in a living room it reads as a hole in the wall during the day.
  • 1% and 3% solar screens if you want the room bright. They are glare tools for a screen, and they are dark to sit behind all day.
  • Faux wood or shutters, if a soft glow is the goal. Louvres slice light into bands rather than diffusing it, which is a look, but it is not this one.
  • A dark light-filtering fabric on a north window. There is not enough light to soften and the room will just feel dim.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Can people see in through a light filtering shade?

Not in daylight, when the outside is brighter than the room. After dark with the lights on, the shade backlights and you get a silhouette. That is physics, not a fabric defect. For night privacy, use a room darkening fabric or a shutter you can tilt.

What is the difference between light filtering and room darkening?

Roughly one stop of light. Light filtering keeps the room bright and glowing. Room darkening drops it to a dim, even light and holds more privacy at night. Both are available on cellular and roller shades.

Which is better for a screen: light filtering fabric or a solar screen?

A solar screen at 1% or 3%, because it cuts the brightness of the sky itself. A light filtering fabric removes the direct beam but leaves a large bright surface, which can still reflect in a monitor. The home office answer is a screen.

Do light filtering fabrics fade?

All fabrics change over years in direct sun. Pale colours show it least. The lining side of a roman shade takes the exposure rather than the face fabric, which is one of the quieter arguments for a lined roman on a south window.

Can I mix light filtering and blackout in one room?

Yes, and on a bedroom with two windows it is often the right call: blackout on the window that faces the street or the sunrise, light filtering on the other. Order swatches of both and hold them up together, because the two fabrics need to agree on colour.
