Vertical Blinds

The practical answer for a sliding door.

Wide vinyl or fabric louvres that rotate for light and slide aside for access. Hard wearing, easy to clean and the least expensive way to cover a big opening.

Why vertical blinds

The widest opening in the house, on one track.

Built for the width, not scaled up to it

A 144" opening is the normal case here, not the limit. The weight hangs from a headrail track instead of a lift cord, so the span does not fight the mechanism.

Walk through without raising anything

Rotate the louvres for light, draw them to one side to use the door. The stack sits clear of the handle.

Louvres are replaceable

A cracked or discoloured louvre is a single part. Vinyl wipes clean; fabric louvres come out for washing.

Child & pet safety

No cord in reach.

Cordless lift is standard on this line, not a paid upgrade. Corded window coverings remain a strangulation hazard for young children, which is why the cordless option is the default here and not an option you have to find.

Guidance published by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends cordless window coverings in any home with young children.

FAQ

Asked often, answered plainly.

If the answer you need is not here, the full list covers every line.

All questions
Vinyl or fabric louvres?

Vinyl for a door that gets used and cleaned. Fabric for a formal room where you want softer light and are willing to vacuum rather than wipe.

What is an S-curve louvre?

A gently curved vinyl profile. It overlaps its neighbour more closely, so you get better light blocking and a softer face than a flat louvre.

Can I replace one broken louvre?

Yes. Louvres clip individually. Order a replacement in the same colour and clip it in.

Do vertical blinds fit a bay window?

Yes, with separate headrails on each face. A single continuous track cannot follow the angle cleanly.
