Overview

Roller and solar shades do one job extremely well: they put a single, flat plane of fabric between you and the sun. Solar screens are rated by openness factor, which is the percentage of the weave that is open. A lower number blocks more glare and heat and gives you more privacy; a higher number keeps more of the view.

Options

What you choose

Openness factors from 1% to 14%

1% and 3% screens are for west-facing rooms and screens you actually work at. 10% and 14% keep the view sharp on a window you care about looking through.

Blackout rollers

A coated fabric with SmartPrivacy® channels blocks light at the edges as well as through the fabric. Best combination we make for a bedroom or media room.

SmartRail™ bottom bar

A weighted, wrapped bottom bar keeps the fabric flat and tracking straight, so the shade does not curl at the hem after a season.

Cordless or motorized

Cordless spring lift on smaller windows, TruQuiet™ motorization on tall or hard-to-reach ones.

Before you order

Measure once, properly.

01

Choose the mount

Inside mount sits within the frame for a built-in look. Outside mount covers the whole opening and blocks more light.

02

Measure width in three places

Top, middle and bottom. Use the narrowest number for an inside mount.

03

Measure height in three places

Left, centre and right. Use the longest number for an inside mount.

04

Do not deduct anything

Give us the exact opening. We make the deductions for you.

The rule that saves most orders: record measurements to the nearest 1/8" and never round down on height. The full walkthrough, with diagrams, is in the measuring guide.

FAQ

4 questions, answered honestly.

Which openness factor should I choose?

Start with the problem. Glare on a screen: 1% or 3%. Heat on a west window: 3% or 5%. A view you want to keep: 10% or 14%. Openness does not equal privacy at night, because a lit room is visible through any screen fabric.

Will a solar shade give me privacy after dark?

No. Screen fabrics reverse at night: with the lights on inside, you are visible from outside. Pair a solar screen with a blackout roller or a drapery panel if you need night privacy.

What is reverse roll?

The fabric comes off the front of the tube instead of the back. Choose it when a deep window frame or a crank handle would otherwise sit in the shade's path.

Can two shades share one headrail?

Yes, on widths above 48" we can build a dual bracket that carries a solar screen and a blackout roller on the same window.
