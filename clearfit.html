Innovation

A headrail cut to your window, not to a size chart.

Most shades are built to the nearest half inch and the gap at the edge is left to the installer. ClearFit™ headrails are cut to your exact opening in 1/8" increments, which is why the light gap is consistent instead of lucky.

Shop at The Home DepotSee compatible products

What the problem actually is

Every inside-mounted shade needs clearance to move. Too little and the shade drags on the frame; too much and you get a bright stripe of daylight down each side. The difference between a shade that looks made for the window and one that looks bought for it is usually a quarter of an inch.

How ClearFit™ works

  • Your measurements are cut in 1/8" increments rather than rounded to the nearest half inch.
  • Deductions are applied by the factory, so you submit the exact opening size and nothing else.
  • Bracket positions are set from the same cut list, so the shade sits square in the opening.
  • The result is a typical edge gap of about 1/4" per side on an inside mount.

Where it matters most

Bedrooms, nurseries and media rooms, where the edge gap is the last light you notice before you fall asleep. Pair ClearFit™ with SmartPrivacy® channels and a blackout fabric for the darkest result we can build.

ClearFit™ is standard on cellular, sheer and Roman shades. It is not a paid upgrade.

Do I need to deduct anything from my measurements?

No. Give us the exact opening in 1/8" increments. If you deduct as well, the shade will be too small.

Does ClearFit™ eliminate the light gap completely?

No, and no inside-mounted shade can. It makes the gap small and consistent. To close it, add SmartPrivacy® channels or use an outside mount.
