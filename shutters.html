Shutters

Architecture, not a window covering.

Interior shutters read as part of the house. Louvres tilt for light and privacy, panels swing open for the full view, and the frame finishes the opening properly.

Why shutters

The only covering that reads as part of the house.

It reads as part of the house

A shutter is framed into the opening and painted like joinery. It is the one window treatment a buyer counts as a fixture rather than a furnishing.

Louvre size sets the character

2 1/2" is traditional and closes tightest. 4 1/2" gives the widest view and the cleanest line on a large opening.

Insulation from the panel itself

A closed hardwood composite panel plus the air gap behind it slows heat at the glass without any fabric in the room.

Child & pet safety

No cord in reach.

Cordless lift is standard on this line, not a paid upgrade. Corded window coverings remain a strangulation hazard for young children, which is why the cordless option is the default here and not an option you have to find.

Guidance published by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission recommends cordless window coverings in any home with young children.

FAQ

Asked often, answered plainly.

If the answer you need is not here, the full list covers every line.

All questions
Which frame do I need?

L-frame if you want the shutter flush inside the opening with no added trim. Deco frame if you want a visible surround, or if the opening is not perfectly square. Both are covered in the installation videos.

Do shutters add value to a home?

They are usually treated as a fixed improvement rather than a furnishing, so they stay with the house at sale. We do not make a specific dollar claim.

Can shutters fit an arched window?

Yes, as a custom shape. Arch, angle and circle configurations are quoted individually.

How do I clean shutters?

Dust the louvres closed in one direction, then flip and dust the other side. A barely damp cloth removes marks. No solvents or furniture polish on a painted finish.
