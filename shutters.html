Overview

A shutter is the only window treatment that becomes part of the architecture. Louvres tilt within a fixed panel for precise light and privacy control, and the whole panel can swing clear when you want the window fully open. Because they are framed into the opening, shutters also seal better than most shades against draughts.

Louvre sizes

2 1/2", 3 1/2" and 4 1/2" louvres. The wider the louvre, the more view you get when open and the fewer lines across the window.

Frame styles

L-frame for a flush, trim-free opening; deco frame for a fuller, more traditional surround. Both have dedicated installation videos.

Tilt options

A front tilt rod for a classic look, or a hidden rear tilt for a clean panel face.

Configurations

Single panel, bi-fold, and bypass tracks for wide openings and patio doors.

Before you order

Measure once, properly.

01

Choose the mount

Inside mount sits within the frame for a built-in look. Outside mount covers the whole opening and blocks more light.

02

Measure width in three places

Top, middle and bottom. Use the narrowest number for an inside mount.

03

Measure height in three places

Left, centre and right. Use the longest number for an inside mount.

04

Do not deduct anything

Give us the exact opening. We make the deductions for you.

The rule that saves most orders: record measurements to the nearest 1/8" and never round down on height. The full walkthrough, with diagrams, is in the measuring guide.

FAQ

4 questions, answered honestly.

Which frame do I need?

L-frame if you want the shutter flush inside the opening with no added trim. Deco frame if you want a visible surround, or if the opening is not perfectly square. Both are covered in the installation videos.

Do shutters add value to a home?

They are usually treated as a fixed improvement rather than a furnishing, so they stay with the house at sale. We do not make a specific dollar claim.

Can shutters fit an arched window?

Yes, as a custom shape. Arch, angle and circle configurations are quoted individually.

How do I clean shutters?

Dust the louvres closed in one direction, then flip and dust the other side. A barely damp cloth removes marks. No solvents or furniture polish on a painted finish.
