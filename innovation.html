Innovation

Five details you only notice when they're missing.

Veneta engineering is not about features on a box. It is about the edge gap you stop seeing, the shade that still tracks straight after five years, and a motor quiet enough to run during a film.

See the productsChild safety standards

ClearFit™

Headrails cut to your exact opening, so light gaps at the edges shrink to a consistent 1/4".

Learn more

SmartRail™

A weighted, fabric-wrapped bottom bar that keeps fabric flat and tracking straight for the life of the shade.

Learn more

SmartPrivacy®

Side channels that close the gap between shade and jamb. The difference between dark and actually dark.

Learn more

TruQuiet™ Motorization

A motor you can run during a film. Rechargeable, schedulable, and quiet enough for a bedroom.

Learn more

RevitaCharge™

Rechargeable battery packs that top up in place, so you are not on a ladder with a pack of AAs.

Learn more
