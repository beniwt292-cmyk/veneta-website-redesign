Home/Innovation/TruQuiet™ Motorization

Innovation

Quiet enough to run during a film.

Most window motors announce themselves. TruQuiet™ uses a damped drive and a soft start and stop, so the shade moves without becoming the loudest thing in the room.

Shop at The Home DepotSee compatible products

What quiet actually means

Two things make a motorized shade annoying: the whine of the motor itself and the clunk at each end of travel. TruQuiet™ addresses both, with a damped gear train and a controlled ramp into and out of every movement.

Power options

  • RevitaCharge™ rechargeable battery pack, topped up in place with a USB-C lead.
  • Hardwired low-voltage supply for new build and renovation.
  • Solar top-up panel for bright windows you would rather not revisit.

Control options

  • Handheld remote, one channel per shade or grouped by room.
  • Wall-mounted keypad for a fixed switch position.
  • ShadeAuto™ Hub for schedules, app control and voice assistants.

Smart-home support depends on your motor and hub combination. Check the compatibility list before you buy. It is the single source of truth and we keep it current.

Where motorization earns its price

Tall windows above a stairwell. A bank of four shades you want to move together. A hot west-facing room where the shades need to close before you get home. Anywhere a manual shade means a ladder.

How long does the battery last?

Typically several months on a full charge, depending on shade size and how often it moves. The remote shows a low-battery warning well before it stops.

Can I add motorization to an existing shade?

No. The motor sits inside the headrail and is fitted during manufacture.

Does it work with Alexa or Google Home?

With the ShadeAuto™ Hub, yes, for supported motor generations. Without the hub, no. The compatibility list states exactly which combinations are supported.
